## What we do

We are an applied-AI studio. Not a research lab and not a body shop. We take a
real business problem, design the system around it, and stay with it until it
runs reliably in production.

- **AI strategy & advisory:** where AI actually pays for itself, and where it does not
- **Generative AI & LLMs:** retrieval pipelines, agents, evaluation harnesses
- **ML engineering:** computer vision, forecasting, classification on real data
- **Data & MLOps:** the pipelines, deployment and monitoring that keep it alive
- **Product engineering:** the web platform around the model, because a model on its own is not a product

## How we build

TypeScript and Python. React, Vite and Tailwind on the front. FastAPI and
Postgres behind. Docker everywhere, nginx in front, Let's Encrypt for TLS,
self-hosted on our own infrastructure so we own the whole stack end to end.
